About

Why this atlas exists.

I've been in IT for 25 years, at Infosys and then as a consultant and fractional executive, across everything from legacy systems to agentic AI.

Every big shift in computing brought patterns that later felt obvious: client-server, service-oriented, cloud-native, event-driven. AI agents are the next shift, and most of the patterns that will define it don't have names yet. This atlas is where I name them, specify them and test them against real industries, in the open.

Everything here is published under CC BY 4.0 (text) and MIT (code and schemas). Use it, adapt it, and credit the source. If you'd like to apply one of these architectures in your organisation, or challenge one, I'd like to hear from you.
